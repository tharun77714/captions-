# Reference Audit: Creator-First Caption Archetypes

## 1. Direct Creator-Caption Competitors

### Captions.ai
- **URL:** https://www.captions.ai/
- **Creator Use Case:** Talking-head mobile videos (TikTok, Reels) requiring maximum retention.
- **Word Grouping:** Dynamic chunking; often 1 word ("viral mode") or 3-5 word phrases.
- **Typography Hierarchy:** Extremely heavy sans-serifs (Montserrat Black, Impact).
- **Active-Word Treatment:** High contrast color pop (e.g., neon yellow/cyan on white) or background highlight box over the spoken word.
- **Positioning:** Locked lower-middle/center, dodging platform UI elements.
- **Motion Principle:** Sudden, bouncy scale punches on active words; rapid color state changes.

### Submagic
- **URL:** https://www.submagic.co/
- **Creator Use Case:** Replicating viral aesthetic "gurus" (Alex Hormozi, Ali Abdaal, MrBeast).
- **Word Grouping:** 1-3 words max per frame for high-energy templates; up to 5 for "Podcast" styles.
- **Typography Hierarchy:** Thick outlines, heavy drop shadows, bold display fonts.
- **Active-Word Treatment:** Multi-color karaoke (e.g., alternating yellow/green/red highlights) + scale punch.
- **Positioning:** Center / Lower-third.

### CapCut (Captions)
- **URL:** https://www.capcut.com/
- **Creator Use Case:** General consumer video editing, massive global reach.
- **Word Grouping:** Standard sentence clause chunking (3-6 words).
- **Typography Hierarchy:** Huge built-in font library, often utilizing background "pill" shapes.
- **Active-Word Treatment:** Karaoke wipe (left-to-right fill) or basic color swap.

### Replix Captions
- **URL:** https://replixcaptions.com/
- **Creator Use Case:** Automated word-by-word captions localized in Telugu, Hindi, and English.
- **Word Grouping:** Standard chunking for Reels.
- **Typography Hierarchy:** Inter and Archivo Black fonts.
- **Active-Word Treatment:** High-contrast yellow and white pops. 
- **What Vidyut Should Learn:** Dedicated native-language capability without manual spelling corrections.
- **What Must Not Be Copied:** Avoid creating a separate app; focus on premium templates that integrate flawlessly.

### Kalakaar
- **URL:** https://kalakaar.io/
- **Creator Use Case:** AI dubbing, translation, and localized subtitles for Indian creators.
- **Word Grouping:** Variable, driven by translated chunk boundaries.
- **Typography Hierarchy:** Clean, readable Indic fonts to support complex ligatures.
- **Active-Word Treatment:** Standard subtitle synchronization over video.
- **What Vidyut Should Learn:** Handling Indic text effectively is a primary technical challenge, requiring specific fonts like Noto Sans Telugu to prevent clipping.

## 2. Motion-Design Inspiration
- **Jitter Community** (https://jitter.video/community): High-end kinetic typography. Values typographic hierarchy.
- **Motionographer / Behance**: Experimental overlapping grids. Shows how text can act as a structural element.

## 3. Anti-Patterns (What to Reject)
1. Movie Title Cards (too slow)
2. Fake News Broadcasts (intrusive)
3. Generic Colored Subtitles (boring)
4. Opaque full-screen backgrounds blocking the creator.
